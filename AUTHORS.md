# Authors and Attribution

## Originator and research director

**Hayden Lindley**

Hayden Lindley originated the QIDS-CAM research proposition: combine interference-inspired destructive reasoning with persistent content-addressed proposition identity so an exact contradicted state can be learned once and reused across every dependent reasoning path.

## Reference implementation

The v0.1.0 research artifact was developed under Hayden Lindley’s direction as the canonical open implementation of the formulation.
