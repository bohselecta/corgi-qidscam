# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project participation should be respectful, evidence-oriented, constructive, and welcoming. Disagreement about novelty, benchmarks, architecture, or interpretation is expected; personal attacks, harassment, intimidation, discriminatory conduct, and deliberate misrepresentation are not acceptable.

## Research integrity

Participants must not:

- fabricate benchmark results;
- hide failed runs that are material to a claim;
- present classical computation as quantum execution;
- remove required attribution;
- knowingly weaken answer-quality gates while advertising efficiency gains;
- misrepresent integrity receipts as proof of real-world truth.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct conflicts with these standards. Security concerns should be reported through the process in `SECURITY.md` rather than disclosed in a way that creates avoidable harm.
